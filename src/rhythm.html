Quick Terms

Pulse

The steady clock you feel underneath the music.

Meter

How pulses group into bars, such as four beats per bar.

Subdivision

How each beat is split into smaller pieces.

Syncopation

Important notes landing between the strongest beat positions.

Swing And Groove

The feel created when offbeats lean late, early, or stay straight.

Phrasing

Where the idea starts, breathes, answers itself, and stops.
