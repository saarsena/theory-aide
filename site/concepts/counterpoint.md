## The one rule that matters most

Most counterpoint guidelines boil down to one idea:
**don't let two voices accidentally fuse.**
The fastest way to fuse them is [[parallel fifths]] and [[parallel octaves]]:
when two voices move in the same direction while staying a fifth or an
octave apart, those intervals are so acoustically "empty" that the voices
lock together. The opposite, [[contrary motion]], is what keeps lines
feeling separate and alive. (Why fifths and octaves in particular? That's
arithmetic. [[math|See the math]].)
